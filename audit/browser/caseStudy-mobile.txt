		73 text Nordtrans's dispatchers were matching proof-of-delivery and CMR scans from email to orders in the TMS by hand, about 220 documents a day. The matches were slow and error-prone, and customers complained about late invoicing. We noticed that this kind of manual matching is a common pattern in freight forwarding, so we started with a conversation about it.
		74 text Velho built the smallest version that could work. An email inbox rule feeds OCR and an order-number regex with a fuzzy fallback on reference and plate. Documents above 0.9 confidence attach automatically; the rest go to a sheet that a person reviews. We skipped the TMS write-back in v1 and used a draft attachment list plus CSV import, because the vendor API had a six-week wait. Handwritten PODs were poor OCR input for about 12% of documents, which is why the human queue exists.
		75 text We piloted with one dispatcher for five days. Adoption spread on its own: all three dispatchers were using it by week four, with a 15-minute weekly review of queue misses. The operations manager's first reaction was positive, and the dispatcher who was skeptical at first asked for the same approach on export documents.
		76 text The measured results are faster handling, fewer wrong attachments and a shorter invoice lead time. The customer happiness the team reports has not been measured yet. Next steps are threshold tuning, miss logging, a Finnish handover document, and possibly export documents such as EUR.1.

		80 text A dispatcher spent 4 minutes on every proof-of-delivery scan. Nordtrans had about 220 a day.

We built a small pipeline: email rule, OCR, order-number matching, and a human queue for anything unsure. In a five-day pilot, 71% of documents matched automatically, handling time dropped from 4.1 to 1.4 minutes, and wrong attachments went from 7.3% to 0.6% in the sample we checked.

The part I like most: the team asked for it on export documents before we suggested it.

We're still measuring customer impact. Pilot numbers are small, so we're tuning thresholds before scaling.

If manual document matching is slowing your invoicing, I'd like to hear how you handle it today.
